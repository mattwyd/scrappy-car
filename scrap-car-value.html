The Short Answer

How Scrap Value Is Calculated

There is no single flat rate. This is what moves your offer up or down.

A scrap car's value starts with its weight, since most of the value is in the metal, then it is adjusted for the rest. A heavier vehicle is usually worth more than a lighter one. Year, make, and model matter because they affect weight and whether parts are worth salvaging. Condition matters too: a running car, a car with usable parts, and a car that is rusted out or missing parts are not priced the same. Finally, the current scrap metal price moves with the market, so the same car can be worth a different amount from one month to the next.

  • Weight: the biggest factor, since metal is priced by weight.
  • Year, make, and model: they set the weight and the parts value.
  • Condition: running, accident-damaged, rusted, flood-damaged, or missing parts.
  • Current scrap prices: the market price on the day you sell.
  • Towing: always free with us, never deducted from your offer.
