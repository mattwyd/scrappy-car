Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

Scrappy Car ("we", "us") buys and removes junk and scrap cars in Scarborough, Pickering and the GTA. This page explains what information we collect when you use scrappycar.ca or call us, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

  • What you give us in the offer form: your first name, phone number, email (optional), your area, details about your vehicle (year, make, model, mileage, condition, keys), any notes, and any photos you upload.
  • Calls: if you call us after clicking one of our Google ads, the call may go through a Google forwarding number, and Google records details such as the call's start time, length and the caller's area code.
  • How you use the site: pages you visit, the site that sent you to us (for example Google or ChatGPT), approximate location, device and browser type, and how far you got in the offer form. We collect this with cookies and similar tools from Google Analytics, Google Ads, PostHog and Vercel Analytics.

How we use it

  • To give you an instant offer and contact you to confirm it and book your pickup.
  • To keep a record of offers, pickups and payments.
  • To understand which ads and pages bring in customers, so we can improve our service and spend our advertising budget well.

Advertising measurement

When you request an offer, our site may send Google a hashed (scrambled, one-way encrypted) copy of the phone number or email you entered. Google uses it only to match your request to an ad click, so we can tell which ads lead to real customers. Google does not receive your name, and we do not use it to show you ads on other sites. You can manage how Google uses your information at myadcenter.google.com.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, and only as needed:

  • HubSpot, to keep our list of offers and customers.
  • Google (Gmail, Google Analytics and Google Ads), to send us new offer alerts and measure our website and ads.
  • PostHog and Vercel, to host the website, store uploaded photos, and measure how the site is used.

Some of these providers store information outside Canada, such as in the United States, where it may be accessed under local law.

How long we keep it

We keep offer and pickup records for as long as we need them to run the business and meet our legal and tax obligations, then delete them.

Your choices

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or tell us to stop contacting you.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The offer form still works without them.
  • You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.

Contact us

For any privacy question or request, call or text (647) 204-4200, reply to any email from us, or write to Scrappy Car, 115 Island Rd, Scarborough, ON. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
