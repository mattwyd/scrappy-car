Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about selling your junk car in Scarborough and the GTA.

How much will I get for my scrap car in Scarborough?+
Cash offers depend on your car's year, make, model, weight, and current scrap metal prices. Most vehicles in the GTA fetch anywhere from $200 to $1,500+. Call us at (647) 204-4200 for a free, no-obligation quote — we'll give you a number right over the phone.
Do you offer free towing in Scarborough and the GTA?+
Yes — towing is always 100% free. We send our tow truck directly to your location at no charge. There are no hidden costs or fees deducted from your cash offer.
Can you pick up my car the same day?+
Absolutely. Same-day pickup is available in most cases across Scarborough, Port Union, West Rouge, Rouge Hill, Highland Creek, and East Toronto. Call us early and we can often be there within a few hours.
What condition does my car need to be in?+
Any condition. Running, non-running, accident-damaged, rusted out, flood-damaged, missing parts — it doesn't matter. We buy cars in all conditions. If it has scrap value, we'll take it.
What documents do I need to sell my junk car in Ontario?+
You'll need your vehicle ownership (permit) to complete the sale. If you've lost it, you can get a replacement from a ServiceOntario location. We'll help guide you through the paperwork and handle the ownership transfer once we arrive.
Do you buy cars without a title or ownership in Ontario?+
Ontario requires a valid ownership document to legally transfer a vehicle. However, if you've misplaced yours, obtaining a replacement is straightforward at ServiceOntario. Call us and we can advise you on the fastest route.
Which areas near Scarborough do you serve?+
We serve all of Scarborough including Port Union, West Rouge, Rouge Hill, and Highland Creek. We also cover East Toronto and extend throughout the Greater Toronto Area including Pickering, Ajax, Markham, Oshawa, and North York. If you're unsure, just call.
How quickly will I receive payment?+
You get cash in hand the moment our driver picks up your vehicle — no waiting, no cheques, no e-transfers. The payment happens right at your location before the car is loaded onto the tow truck.
Is scrap car removal environmentally responsible?+
Yes. We follow all provincial regulations for vehicle disposal. Fluids are properly drained and recycled, reusable parts are salvaged, and the remaining metal is sent to licensed recycling facilities. It's the greenest way to retire an old vehicle.
