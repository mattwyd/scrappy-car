The Short Answer

What To Do If You Have Lost Your Ownership

This is the fastest route back to a sale you can complete.

  1. Call us first: tell us about the car and that the ownership is missing. We will advise you on the fastest route for your situation.
  2. Get a replacement at ServiceOntario: a lost vehicle ownership (permit) can be replaced at a ServiceOntario location.
  3. Schedule pickup: once you have the ownership in hand, we can pick up the car, usually the same day, and pay cash on the spot.

Do not let a missing ownership sit for months. The replacement step is the only holdup in most cases, and the car itself can be in any condition: running, non-running, accident-damaged, rusted, or flood-damaged.
